The question

What makes a system genuinely capable of liberation rather than merely efficient at reproducing its own forms of capture?

01 · Canonical framework

Exergism

Emergentist Metaphysics of Liberation is a philosophical system centered on emergence, truth, consciousness, autonomy, exergy, capture, liberation, and the possibility of breaking recurrent cycles of domination.

A

Canonical corpus

The philosophical source is maintained as a version-controlled canonical corpus rather than dispersed across secondary explanations.

B

Formal analysis

A separate formal exergic-analysis model makes variables, uncertainty, temporal integration, and non-compensatory rules explicit.

C

Semantic projection

An OWL projection is generated from canonical source artifacts and must remain reproducible from them.

Current public line: Exergism v0.1.0

The project remains on the 0.x line. Formal ambiguities are recorded rather than silently normalized.

03 · Engineering constraints

Make authority inspectable.

The commons is being built around a small set of structural constraints rather than opaque institutional discretion.

  1. 01

    Explicit

    Normative, legal, and technical effects should arise from identifiable artifacts and procedures, not implication.

  2. 02

    Versioned

    Released artifacts and decisions must be attributable to exact versions rather than silently rewritten by later state.

  3. 03

    Contestable

    Evidence, assumptions, classifications, and governance outcomes should remain open to challenge and adversarial review.

  4. 04

    Machine-auditable

    Where practical, invariants should be expressed in schemas, semantic models, validation rules, tests, and reproducible generation.

  5. 05

    Non-retroactive

    Living knowledge can improve without silently changing the legal or semantic meaning of immutable historical releases.

04 · Web architecture

Identity is not presentation.

www.exergism.org is the public-facing website.

id.exergism.org is the persistent semantic identifier authority.

Keeping those roles separate means the website can evolve freely without forcing changes to stable identifiers embedded in datasets, ontologies, specifications, or citations.
